# Noa ad (AI character), 9:16, English + Hebrew

Published at https://alidealer.com/ads (site/ad/alidealer-ad-noa-{en,he}.mp4).

- s1–s5.png: scenes made with `openkbs image` from Noa's portrait plus the product photos.
- clip_s1–s5.mp4: moving clips from the free Wan 2.2 Hugging Face Space (wan-noa.mjs; reads an optional HF_TOKEN for more daily quota, SEED to vary a take). Scenes without a clip get a slow push-in on the still.
- vo/: Noa's voice lines (voice "Kore"), 24 kHz WAV.
- make.mjs: builds the ads with ffmpeg (expects the files in /tmp/noa, voices in /tmp/vid, fonts in /tmp/vid; music from marketing/ad-video/music.wav).
