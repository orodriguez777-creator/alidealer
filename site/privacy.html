Privacy policy

Effective October 13, 2026

This policy explains what information www.Alidealer.com (the website at alidealer.com and the www.Alidealer.com Android app, together "the service") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. In short: we do not ask who you are, we do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not sell or share personal information.

Who we are

www.Alidealer.com is a deals site run by an individual. It lists hand-picked products from AliExpress and links to them. For any privacy question, contact orodriguez777@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • No account, no sign-up. You can use the whole service without giving us your name, email, phone number or any other personal details.
  • Deal clicks. When you tap "See on AliExpress", we count that the deal was clicked and when. We do not record your IP address, device, location or any identifier with that count, so it cannot be linked to you. Clicks from search engine robots are not counted. Click counts are used only to see which deals are popular.
  • Technical logs. The service is hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS) in the European Union. Like any web server, AWS may briefly keep standard technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) to deliver pages and protect against abuse. We do not use these logs to identify or profile visitors.

Information stored on your device

  • Language choice. If you switch between English and Hebrew, that choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it. It never leaves your device.
  • Offline files. The app and the installed website keep a copy of the site's own pages and images on your device so they open faster and show a page when you are offline.
  • No cookies from us. The service itself does not set cookies and does not use advertising or analytics tools.

Third-party services

Some content is loaded from other companies, which receive your IP address and browser details when your device fetches it:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) for the text fonts. See the Google privacy policy.
  • AliExpress image servers for product photos.

When you share a deal using the WhatsApp, Telegram or Facebook buttons, or your phone's share menu, the chosen service opens and its own privacy policy applies.

Affiliate links and AliExpress

Links to products are AliExpress affiliate links. When you follow one, you leave our service and go to AliExpress, which may set its own cookies to record that you came from us, so that we may earn a small commission if you buy. This costs you nothing extra. We do not receive your name, address, payment details or order contents from AliExpress; we only see commission totals. Your use of AliExpress is covered by the AliExpress privacy policy.

How we use and share information

We use the limited information above only to run the service, keep it secure and see which deals are popular. We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, and we do not share it with anyone except the hosting and service providers named above, or where the law requires it.

Data retention

Daily click counts are kept for 30 days, plus an overall count per deal. Neither contains any personal information. Your language choice and offline files stay on your device until you clear the site's data or uninstall the app.

Security

All traffic to and from the service is encrypted with HTTPS. Because we collect almost no personal information, there is very little that could be exposed.

Children

The service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the Israeli Privacy Protection Law or the EU GDPR), you may have the right to ask what information we hold about you, to correct it or to delete it. Since we do not keep personal information linked to you, there is usually nothing to return or delete, but you are welcome to write to orodriguez777@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy: orodriguez777@gmail.com.
